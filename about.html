About YES-O

Discover our mission, meet our team, and learn about our journey in environmental stewardship

Introduction

The Youth for Environment in Schools Organization

YES-O is a vibrant, student-led organization established to promote environmental awareness among young people. We believe that students have the power to drive meaningful change in their communities through informed action, collaboration, and innovation.

From tree planting initiatives to science fairs, from coastal cleanups to environmental research, our members engage in a wide range of activities that develop leadership skills while making a tangible impact on the environment.

With chapters in schools across the region, YES-O has grown into a movement of young environmental advocates committed to building a sustainable future.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to become environmental stewards, fostering a culture of sustainability and community service in every school.

Our Vision

A generation of environmentally conscious youth leading the way toward a sustainable and resilient future for all.

Our Objectives

  • Promote environmental awareness and action among students
  • Foster leadership and teamwork through community projects
  • Build partnerships with environmental organizations
  • Create sustainable school environments
Our Journey

History & Achievements

From a small club to a recognized environmental organization

YES-O was founded in 2018 by a group of passionate students who wanted to make a difference in their school's environmental impact. What started as a small club with 15 members has grown into one of the most active student organizations in the region, with hundreds of members and dozens of successful projects.

2018
YES-O founded with 15 charter members. First tree planting activity conducted with 100 seedlings.
2019
Launched the Plastic-Free Campus campaign. Membership grew to 80 students.
2020
Adapted to pandemic with virtual environmental education sessions. Launched online advocacy campaigns reaching 5,000+ students.
2022
Returned to full operations. Established the School Botanical Garden. Partnered with 3 local environmental NGOs.
2024
Membership surpassed 500 active students. Launched the Rainwater Harvesting project. Featured in national education summit.
2026
Expanded to 5 partner schools. Over 12,500 trees planted cumulatively.
