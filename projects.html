Flagship Project

Plastic-Free Campus Initiative

Our flagship project aims to eliminate single-use plastics across all school premises. Through awareness campaigns, alternative solutions, and policy advocacy, we've reduced plastic waste by 78% in two years. The project includes installation of water refilling stations, distribution of reusable bags, and a comprehensive recycling program.
